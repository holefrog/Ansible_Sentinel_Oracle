# Privacy Policy (隐私政策)

*Last updated: November 2026*

**Stock Sentinel** is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled when you interact with the Service.

---

### 1. Zero Personal Data Collection (不收集任何个人数据)
- **We do not collect, store, log, or sell any personal data.**
- The Service does not request or retain personally identifiable information (PII), such as real names, email addresses, IP addresses, or payment details.
- Interactions (such as Discord slash commands) are processed in real-time in memory and are discarded immediately after execution.

### 2. What Data Is Processed (仅处理公开数据与运行缓存)
- **Public Market & News Data:** The system monitors public financial news feeds (RSS), publicly available market metrics, and quotes.
- **Local Technical Caches:** The project uses a local database solely for operational efficiency (e.g., deduplicating public news IDs, recording LLM token consumption statistics). None of this cached data contains user identity or personal data.

### 3. Third-Party Integrations (第三方服务)
The Service relies on third-party APIs and platforms (e.g., Discord, financial data providers, LLM gateways):
- Messages and commands submitted via Discord are subject to the [Discord Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy).
- Requests to upstream AI/financial APIs contain only market tickers or public news snippets, never personal user data.

### 4. Data Security (数据安全)
Since no personal data is collected or retained on our servers, there is no personal user data at risk of exposure or breach.

### 5. Contact & Questions (联系方式)
If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please submit an issue through the project's repository.
